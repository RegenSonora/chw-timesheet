# Signing and sending an invoice or timesheet

_For contractors, employees, reviewers and supervisors at Regenerating Sonora, Inc. The app does not sign anything; signing happens in the email and PDF apps you already have (SPEC §8)._

## Invoices (contractors)

1. **Create the invoice in the app.** On Home, tap **Generate Regen Invoice**, check the preview, then tap **Create invoice files**.
2. **Share the files to yourself.** Tap **Share files** and email all four files to yourself: the invoice PDF, a picture (PNG) of the invoice page, the timesheet CSV and the entry log CSV. On iPhone Mail, choose **Actual Size** if it asks about image size.
3. **Sign the PDF, or sign the picture.** Sign one of them, not both.
   - **PDF on iPhone:** open the PDF from your email, tap the **Markup** button (pen icon), add your signature in the Contractor box, tap **Done**.
   - **PDF on Android:** open the PDF in **Adobe Acrobat Reader**, tap **Fill & Sign**, add your signature in the Contractor box, save.
   - **Picture on iPhone:** save the picture to Photos, open it, tap **Edit**, then **Markup** (pen icon), sign in the Contractor box, tap **Done**.
   - **Picture on Android:** open the picture in **Google Photos** (or your gallery app), tap **Edit**, then **Markup**, sign in the Contractor box, save a copy.
4. **Email the signed file and both CSV files to your reviewer.** Send all three in one email. A signed picture goes at **Actual Size**.
5. **The reviewer signs "Services received"** on the same file the same way and emails it with both CSV files to the office. Usually the reviewer is the CHW Team Lead. When the Team Lead is the person invoicing, someone else signs, as the office decides.
6. **The office signs its lines on paper.**

## Timesheets (employees)

1. **Create the timesheet in the app.** On Home, tap **Submit timesheet**, check the preview, then tap **Create timesheet files**.
2. **Share the files to yourself**, as for invoices: the timesheet PDF, a picture (PNG) of the timesheet page, the timesheet CSV and the entry log CSV.
3. **Sign the PDF, or sign the picture,** in the **Employee** box on the first page (the TIMESHEET page), the same way as above. Sign one, not both.
4. **Email the signed file and both CSV files to your supervisor.**
5. **Your supervisor signs the same page** in the **Supervisor signature** box below yours, writes their name and the date, and emails it with both CSV files to the office. The supervisor signs the file you sent, not a new copy.
6. **The office signs its lines on paper.**

## Rules

- **Email only.** Send invoices by email, not by text message, chat apps or shared folders.
- **Sign one file.** Either the PDF or the picture, never both, and everyone signs that same file. The picture has the same fingerprint and QR code as the PDF.
- **Never edit a signed file.** If something is wrong, don't change the PDF or the CSV files. Tell your reviewer or supervisor, and make the correction as a new invoice or timesheet from the app.
- **The office keeps the final PDF with its email thread.** The email thread shows who signed and when.
- **Need the files again?** In the app, open **Past invoices** (or **Past timesheets**) on Home, tap it and share it again. The files are the same as the first time.

## Don't lose your hours

Entries are saved only on your phone, and there is no backup. **Removing the app from your Home Screen (iPhone) or clearing the site's data in Chrome (Android) erases every entry that isn't invoiced or submitted yet.** Once an invoice or timesheet is emailed, that work is safe in email. Invoice or submit often.
