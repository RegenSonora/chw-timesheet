# 0021 — Regen Time Tracking icon: desert night scene

- **Status:** Accepted by Chris, November 2, 2026. For Regen Time Tracking; Historic keeps decision 0017.
- **Spec:** SPEC §2 ("Logo"), §10

## Decision

The icon is a flat desert scene drawn from the Regenerating Sonora sketch, filling the square (the phone rounds the corners):

- a starry night sky `#1b2150` with cream stars `#fbf3de` and a full moon rising over the red-brown mesa `#7a3b26`;
- a green slope `#2e8b6a`, sand `#f2dc9b` and turquoise water `#5ec4c8`;
- the sun `#f28c28` setting behind the purple mountain in front (`#5a4fa3`, shaded side `#3e3680`).

The Android maskable icon draws the scene at 75% so the moon and sun stay inside the safe zone. It is the Home Screen icon only; inside the app there is still no logo.

## Why

After the split (decision 0020), the two apps need icons that are easy to tell apart. Of the mockups (scene in a circle, full scene, clock ring, egg), Chris chose the full scene, then the night version with the moon and the setting sun.
