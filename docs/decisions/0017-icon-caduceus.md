# 0017 — Home Screen icon: caduceus in an egg

- **Status:** Accepted by Chris, November 1, 2026. Replaces decision 0015. Since the split (decision 0020) it applies to CHW Time Tracker Historic only; Regen Time Tracking uses decision 0021.
- **Spec:** SPEC §2 ("Logo"), §10 ("Neutral styling")

## Decision

The app icon is a green caduceus (staff, two snakes, wings) inside a cream egg, on green filling the square; the phone rounds the corners.

- Green `#2f7d4a`, cream `#f6f3e8`.
- The Android maskable icon draws the egg at 80% so it stays inside the safe zone.
- It is the Home Screen icon only. Inside the app there is still no logo (SPEC §2), and the accent stays blue `#1f5fc8`.

## Why

After the first phone tests, Chris chose a health emblem over the letters "CHW". Of the four mockups (cream egg, white outline, two greens, rod of Asclepius), the cream egg keeps the emblem readable at Home Screen size.
