# 0015 — Home Screen icon

- **Status:** Accepted by Chris, November 1, 2026
- **Spec:** SPEC §2 ("Logo"), §10 ("Neutral styling")

## Decision

The app icon is the white letters **CHW** on the accent blue `#1f5fc8`, filling the square; the phone rounds the corners. It is not a logo; there is still no logo (SPEC §2). The Android maskable icon keeps the letters inside the safe zone.

## Why

The spec fixes the colors and rules out a logo but does not say what the icon shows. Letters are easy to find among other apps.
